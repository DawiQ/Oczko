## Full description (4000)

Oczko is the Polish game of 21, the way families play it at home. Everyone in
turn says “Draw” or “Stay”. Closest to 21 wins the round — go over and you
are bust. Two aces? Perskie oczko — the best hand of all!

You play for points: each round won is a point, a match goes to 5, 10 or 15.
No money, no prizes — just cards and a good time.

YOUR HOUSE RULES
• Ready rule sets, among them Pontoon: face cards 10, the ace 1 or 11,
  a pontoon beats any 21 and five cards beat any total.
• Family game (everyone against everyone) or with a banker.
• Face card values (J 2, Q 3, K 4 or your own), a deck of 52, 32 or 24 cards.
• Two aces on or off, the ace as 11 or as 1 or 11, one or two starting
  cards, a hand limit, rules for ties and the bank.
Set them once — the first time you open the app — and change them whenever
you like.

EIGHT CHARACTERS AT THE TABLE
From Zoe, who is just starting out, to careful Hattie and daredevil Vince,
all the way to the Professor and the Count, who work out the chances before
every move. Levels tuned over thousands of played rounds.

COACH AND ANALYSIS
The coach shows your chance to bust and how likely you are to win if you
stay or draw. After a match, watch the replay move by move with a review of
your decisions.

WITH FRIENDS OVER WI‑FI
Everyone on their own phone, on the same network — no internet, no
accounts. The host opens a table; the others join from the list, with a
code or a QR code.

PROFILE
Ranks from Nine up to Two aces, badges, statistics.

OCZKO PRO
Unlimited coach and analysis, tables for 7–8 people, 50 matches in your
history, export, extended statistics, decks, felts and backs.
The full game is free — Pro changes nothing in how the game is played.
